Undercurrent

Privacy

Short version: Undercurrent never sees what you type, and nothing it learns leaves your machine.

The VS Code extension

  • It notices that the file in front of you changed, and when you scroll or move around. It never reads the contents of your edits.
  • It remembers the length of your recent thinking pauses and the start and end times of your flow sessions, so it can tune itself and show you today's totals.
  • That information is stored in VS Code's local storage on your computer. It is not sent anywhere.
  • All music is generated on your computer. Nothing is streamed unless you set your own stream URL, in which case your editor connects to the address you chose.

This website

  • The demo runs entirely in your browser. What you type in it is not sent anywhere.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of serving them.
  • The site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs.
